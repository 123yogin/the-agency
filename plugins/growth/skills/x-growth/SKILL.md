# X Growth

Growth on X comes from being worth replying to, consistently, in one niche.
Read `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` first if it's a company account.

## Hard rules

- **No fabricated results, numbers or "I did X" stories.** Ask for the real ones.
- **No engagement farming:** no follow-for-follow, reply-guy spam, giveaway
  bait, mass-tagging, or coordinated "engagement pods". They violate X's
  platform-manipulation rules and erode trust.
- **Reach claims are hypotheses.** X changes ranking often; say "tends to",
  test, and judge by the account's own analytics.
- **One idea per post.** If it needs two, it's a thread or two posts.

## 1. Profile audit

- [ ] First line of bio: who you help or what you make, specifically
      ("I build Postgres tools for small teams", not "builder | thinker").
- [ ] One credibility element that's true (role, project, result).
- [ ] One link that goes somewhere useful (newsletter, product, site).
- [ ] Pinned post: recent, your best work or clearest statement of what you post about.
- [ ] Header and avatar consistent with other profiles.
- [ ] Last 30 posts: one recognisable niche? a mix of posts, threads, replies, quotes?

## 2. Niche research

1. Find 5–10 accounts in the niche with strong engagement relative to size
   (web search, X search with `min_faves:` operators, lists).
2. For each: themes that perform, hook patterns, format mix, how they reply.
3. Note gaps: questions people keep asking that nobody answers well.

## 3. Formats

**Single post** — one observation, lesson, framework, opinion, or result.
Shorter usually reads better; line breaks for rhythm.
Shapes: "[Thing] is underrated. Here's why:" · "I did X for N months. Biggest
lesson:" · "[Concept] in 30 seconds:" · "Unpopular opinion: …" (only if you
actually hold it and can defend it).

**Thread**
```
1  Hook: the payoff in one line; signal a thread follows
2  Context or promise
3…n One idea per post, each understandable alone, 2–4 lines
Last Summary + one CTA (follow, newsletter, product) — not all three
```
Usually 5–12 posts. Cut any weak post; one dull post loses readers for the rest.

**Quote post** — add what the original missed: data, a counterpoint, a
first-hand example. Never just "This".

**Replies** — the fastest path to visibility for a small account. Reply to
larger accounts in the niche with something that stands alone as a good post.
Be early on posts where you have real expertise.

## 4. Distribution habits

- Links: many observe that posts with external links get less reach; test
  link-in-reply vs link-in-post on your own account.
- Stick around after posting and reply to early replies — conversation
  (especially the author replying back) is what X's ranking has historically
  weighted most (per X's 2023 open-sourced ranking code; may have changed).
- At most one or two hashtags, only if the community uses them.
- Don't tag people who aren't part of the post.
- Consistency over volume: pick a cadence you can sustain for 3 months.

## 5. Plan (first 4–8 weeks)

1. Fix profile and pinned post.
2. Pick 2–3 content pillars inside one niche.
3. Pick ~20 accounts to engage with genuinely.
4. Daily: a few thoughtful replies; one post. Weekly: one thread.
5. After 2–3 weeks: review what earned replies and profile visits (X
   analytics), double down on the top two formats, drop the rest.
6. Repurpose winners into LinkedIn posts or newsletter issues (adapted, not copied).

```

## Pillars

<drafts, with character counts>

## What to measure
Replies, profile visits, follows per post, link clicks — reviewed weekly.
```
